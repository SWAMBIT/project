1 DEC2025 Launch
1 December 2025

SWAMBIT officially starts

SWAMBIT began on 1 December 2025 as one of the 11 pilot projects of the BMFTR-funded NeuroAI (NI:KI) initiative.

Research focus

SWAMBIT builds AI the way the brain is organised: many small, sparsely active specialists instead of one dense monolith, coordinated through a shared workspace and enriched by a hippocampus-like memory. The goal is models that are more efficient and easier to extend.

  • Specialist Modulescortical macro-columnsSmall, interpretable transformers per modality and task; only a sparse few are active at a time.
  • Shared Global WorkspacethalamusA sparse write bottleneck where modules share one representational space.
  • Memory BenchhippocampusKnowledge lives in a key-value store outside the weights, queried through the workspace.
